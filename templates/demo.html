AI Detection Pipeline

End-to-end flow from raw claim data to actionable routing decision

1

Data Ingestion & Feature Engineering

4,500 records loaded. Date fields converted to ClaimMonth, DayOfWeek, Year. One-hot encoding applied to 7 categorical features. Non-predictive IDs dropped.

2

SMOTE Oversampling — Imbalance Correction

Original: 4,230 Legitimate vs 270 Fraud (94:6). SMOTE generates synthetic fraud samples. After resampling: balanced 3,384 vs 3,384 training set.

Fraud samples: 270 → 3,384
3

XGBoost Classifier Training

300 gradient-boosted trees, depth 6, lr 0.05. Outputs a continuous fraud probability 0.0–1.0 for every claim. Trained on SMOTE-balanced data.

ROC-AUC: {{ metrics.roc_auc }} · PR-AUC: {{ metrics.pr_auc }}
4

Three-Tier Risk Routing Engine — Gap 2

Dual thresholds θ₁=0.30 and θ₂=0.60 partition the probability space: Auto-Approve (fast-track genuine) / Manual Review / Investigate (FIU). Eliminates binary all-or-nothing decisions.

θ₁ = 0.30 · θ₂ = 0.60
5

SHAP Explainability — Gap 3

SHapley Additive exPlanations computed per-claim. Claims adjusters see why each claim was flagged before any adverse decision is made. No black-box outcomes.

Research Gap 2 — Three-Tier Risk Routing Engine

Binary 0/1 output floods investigators or approves everything. Dual-threshold routing solves this.

Tier 1 — Auto-Approve
Fraud Score < 30%
θ₁ = 0.30
Genuine claims fast-tracked. No human delay. Policyholder satisfaction and trust maintained.
Tier 2 — Manual Review
30% ≤ Score < 60%
θ₁ → θ₂
Human adjuster reviews SHAP evidence. Not rejected automatically — balances speed with diligence.
Tier 3 — Investigate (FIU)
Score ≥ 60%
θ₂ = 0.60
Immediate FIU escalation with full SHAP audit report attached for investigator.
